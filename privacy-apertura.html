Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Apertura ("we", "our", or "the app") is a movie and series tracker developed by Sokyra Labs. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Watchlists stay local

Want to Watch, Watching and Watched all live on your device via SharedPreferences — nothing personal touches our servers.

No data sold

Titles come from TMDB, ads from AdMob, purchases through RevenueCat — never sold or traded to anyone else.

Searches, not identities

Movie and series requests go to TMDB without any personal data attached.

Delete anytime

Clearing app data or uninstalling removes your entire watchlist for good.

01

Data We Collect

Apertura stores your watchlists (Want to Watch, Watching, Watched) locally on your device. We do not collect or store your personal data on our servers.

The following information may be processed:

Watchlist data
Movies and series you save to your lists. Stored locally only via SharedPreferences.
Movie & series data
Titles, posters, and ratings are fetched from the TMDB API (The Movie Database) based on your searches and browsing. No personal data is sent to TMDB.
Advertising data
The app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.
Purchase data
If you subscribe to Apertura Premium, purchases are processed by Google Play and verified via RevenueCat. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
02

How We Use Data

  • To provide core app functionality (watchlists, search, recommendations)
  • To fetch movie and series content from TMDB
  • To display relevant advertisements via AdMob
  • To process and verify Premium subscriptions via RevenueCat
03

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • TMDB API — search queries and content requests (no personal data)
  • Google AdMob — advertising identifiers for ad delivery
  • RevenueCat — purchase verification for Premium subscriptions

If you have Weekloom installed on the same device, Apertura can expose the titles, media type and rating of what you watched that week to it through a local, on-device connection. This never leaves your device and involves no network transmission.

04

Data Storage and Security

Your watchlist data is stored locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences. You can delete all data at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

05

Children's Privacy

Apertura is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

06

Your Rights

You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in your device settings. For any privacy-related requests, contact us at the email below.

07

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
