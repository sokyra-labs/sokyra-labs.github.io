Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

CareDose ("we", "our", or "the app") is a medication reminder and tracking application developed by Sokyra Labs. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

On-device by default

Medications, profiles and dose history are stored locally — not on our servers.

AI-assisted scanning

A package photo is relayed through our server to Google Gemini to read it — the photo is never stored afterward.

Interaction checks via RxNav

Only medication names are sent to the U.S. National Library of Medicine's public lookup service — no identifiers attached.

Delete anytime

Uninstalling removes everything — there's nothing left on our side to ask us to delete.

01

Data We Collect

Your medication data stays locally on your device. We do not collect or store your medication history, doses, or profiles on our servers. The following information may be processed:

Medication data
Medication names, dosages, schedules, and intake history you enter. Stored locally only.
Profile data
Profile names and colors you create within the app. Stored locally only.
App settings
Notification preferences and display settings. Stored locally only.
Package scan photos
If you use the "Scan package" feature, the photo you take is sent to our server (a Cloudflare Worker), which forwards it to Google's Gemini API to read the medication name, dosage, and pill count. The photo is not stored by us — it is used only to generate the result, which is returned to your device.
Medication names for interaction checks
If you have two or more active medications, their names are sent to RxNav, a public drug-interaction lookup service operated by the U.S. National Library of Medicine (NIH), to check for known interactions. No other personal data is included in this request.
Advertising data
The app uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.
02

How We Use Data

  • To display your medication schedules and send local reminder notifications
  • To track dose history and calculate adherence statistics
  • To read medication details from a package photo via Google Gemini, when you use the scan feature
  • To check for interactions between your active medications via RxNav
  • To generate PDF and CSV reports that you can share from your device
  • To display advertisements via Google AdMob
03

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • Google Gemini API — package photos are relayed through our own server to Gemini for medication recognition. Photos are not stored on our server or by us afterward.
  • RxNav (U.S. National Library of Medicine) — medication names only, to check for drug interactions. No account or device identifier is included.
  • Google AdMob — advertising identifiers for ad delivery.

When you choose to export a report (PDF or CSV), the file is created locally on your device and shared only through the standard system share dialog — entirely under your control.

04

Data Storage and Security

All medication, profile, and settings data is stored on your device using the standard Android local storage (SharedPreferences). You can delete all app data at any time by clearing app data or uninstalling the app from your device settings. Package photos used for scanning are transmitted for processing only and are not retained by us.

05

Camera & Photo Library Permission

CareDose requests access to your camera and photo library exclusively for the package scan feature — to identify a medication from a photo of its packaging. Access is only requested when you tap the scan option, and photos are only sent to our server for that specific request.

06

Notifications Permission

CareDose requests permission to send local notifications in order to deliver medication reminders. Notifications are generated entirely on-device and do not involve any external servers or services.

07

Children's Privacy

CareDose is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

08

Your Rights

You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in your device settings. Since all medication and profile data is stored locally, uninstalling the app permanently removes all your information. Package photos and interaction-check requests are not retained by us, so there is nothing further to delete on our side.

09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
