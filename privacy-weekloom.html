Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Weekloom ("we", "our", or "the app") is a life tracker application developed by Sokyra Labs. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

On-device by default

Your date of birth, mood entries and notes are stored locally — never transmitted to our servers.

No data sold

Only AdMob (ads), Google Play (purchases) and IP-based geolocation (weather) are involved — never sold or traded.

One-time purchase

Remove Ads is a single Google Play transaction — no subscription, no account required.

Delete anytime

Clearing app data or uninstalling removes every entry and setting for good.

01

Data We Collect

Weekloom stores all your personal data locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit your personal data to our servers.

The following information may be processed:

Date of birth and lifespan
Used to calculate your life grid. Stored locally only via SharedPreferences.
Weekly entries
Mood ratings and personal notes you add to each week. Stored locally in an SQLite database on your device.
Approximate location (for weather)
To show the week's weather, your device's IP address is sent to a third-party IP-geolocation service to estimate an approximate location. No GPS location is used, and nothing is stored on our servers.
Weekly data from other Sokyra Labs apps (on-device only)
If Kapva, Ignis and/or Apertura are installed on the same device, Weekloom can read a small weekly summary from each — a financial total (type, currency, amount) from Kapva, activity totals (workouts, calories, water) from Ignis, and watched titles from Apertura — through a local, on-device connection. This never leaves your device and involves no network transmission.
Advertising data
The app uses Google AdMob to display ads (if you have not removed ads). AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.
Purchase data
If you purchase the "Remove Ads" option, the transaction is processed by Google Play. See Google Play's Privacy Policy.
02

How We Use Data

  • To display your personalised life grid based on your date of birth
  • To store and display your weekly mood entries and notes
  • To show insights and statistics about your documented weeks
  • To show the week's weather based on an approximate, IP-based location
  • To display weekly summaries from Kapva, Ignis or Apertura, if installed on the same device
  • To display advertisements via Google AdMob (removed after purchase)
  • To process one-time purchases via Google Play
03

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • Google AdMob — advertising identifiers for ad delivery (only if ads are active)
  • Google Play — purchase verification for the "Remove Ads" feature
  • A third-party IP-geolocation service — your IP address, to estimate an approximate location for the weather feature
04

Data Storage and Security

All your entries (mood ratings, notes, date of birth) are stored locally on your device using SQLite and SharedPreferences. No data is uploaded to any server. You can delete all data at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

05

Children's Privacy

Weekloom is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

06

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in your device settings.

07

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
